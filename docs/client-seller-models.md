# Primary client model

IntoFocus classifies each brand by how it sells on Amazon. The class is called the seller type. It decides which retail question the dashboard is for.

**Featured Offer** is Amazon's name for the Buy Box. BB in the questions below means Featured Offer.

Fender is **Partner-led**.

How each type is drawn on the dashboard is not decided. The questions below are the intent. They are not a spec for the current canvas.

| Model | Description | Primary dashboard question |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Partner-led | Brand does not sell directly. Amazon Retail and authorized 3P partners sell its products. | What percentage of listings has a healthy BB controlled by an authorized seller? |
| Brand-led marketplace | Brand sells through its own Amazon seller account, possibly alongside other sellers. | What percentage of BBs does the brand own, and who is taking the rest? |
| Amazon Retail only | Brand wholesales to Amazon Retail and expects Amazon to sell the products. | Where is Amazon Retail losing the BB, going out of stock, or facing unauthorized competition? |
| Hybrid | Brand uses some combination of Amazon Retail, its own seller account, and authorized 3P partners. | Is each listing controlled by the correct seller for that product's channel strategy? |
| No managed Amazon presence | Brand does not intentionally sell on Amazon or lacks control of its Amazon presence. | Who is selling the products, who controls Brand Registry, and what opportunity or risk does Amazon represent? |
