## What this dashboard is

This dashboard shows how Fender appears in AI answers and on Amazon. IntoFocus reports on one brand per dashboard, and Fender is the pilot brand. On Amazon, Fender is Partner-led: authorized resellers sell it, and Fender doesn't sell there itself.

The primary retail question is this: does an authorized seller hold a healthy Featured Offer on each active listing? It can't be answered yet, because no authorized-seller list is stored.

## Who it is for

It's built equally for you and for the people who run ecommerce and marketing day to day. Each area opens with a chart, and every figure opens an explainer.

## How ecommerce changes AI search

Your ecommerce pages are where an assistant can read your product facts. Of 947 checked listings, a fender.com page was found for 141 (14.9%). All 141 lack the additionalProperty block, where detailed specs would go, so an assistant has no spec facts to read there.

Fender or Squier was named in 3,209 of 3,885 resolved AI answers (82.6%) across ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Gemini. Beginner guitars are the weakest category: Fender or Squier in 168 of 287 (58.5%), and Yamaha in 111 of 287 (38.7%). And 222 of 4,287 answers stated a wrong spec. The dashboard puts the two sides next to each other and doesn't claim a cause, because no stored reading yet ties a listing's gap to an answer.

## What you see first

You start on a map: a hub for Fender, plus six areas (AI Search Visibility, Portfolio Retail, AI Readiness, Competitive Radar, Action Items, and Estimates).

On Amazon, 3,604 listings are monitored and 1,007 have an active offer. Of the 438 active offers where Amazon's benchmark, the Competitive External Price, has been read so far, 48 have the Featured Offer withheld while priced above that benchmark. Those 48 sit $10,667.03 above the benchmark in total. That's a price gap, not revenue. For 19 of them, a stored Musician's Friend price equals the benchmark, so you see the matching outside listing.

## Why you can trust a number

Every figure comes from a stored reading or a stored formula in one registry. Open any figure to see its meaning, as-of date, coverage, formula, inputs, and source. Unfinished reads say "not final" and show the count. Missing inputs say "unavailable," never zero or a typed number. That's why the old dashboard's two dollar estimates ($680K and $38M to $62M) show as unavailable: they have no stored inputs. The header shows the oldest as-of date.

## What it asks you to do

There are 54 open action items. Your team owns 48 of them, one per listing with a withheld Featured Offer: match the outside price or correct the outside listing. You also owe the authorized-seller list and the MAP sheet. IntoFocus owns adding additionalProperty to the 141 found fender.com pages and finishing the seller read for 67 active offers. Inputs for the two estimates are open too.
